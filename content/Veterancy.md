# Veterancy

> Ork, Necron, SM, Ad Mech all get +10% per level.
> A level 0 unit of 2500hp will be 2750 at lvl1, and 5000hp at lvl 10.
> Space marines also get bonus damage to go with it: 5% per level, for +50% at lvl 10.
> Orks earn xp much faster by default. But all units have different xp requirements per level, as well as damage to xp ratios.
 -- Artist

> [!quote] Elliott
Unit veterancy is primarily gained the same way across all factions: actions such as engaging in combat grant experience, increasing a unit’s veterancy level at certain thresholds. **Veterancy increases a unit’s maximum HP and their damage output.** Certain units may also have wargear and abilities locked behind veterancy and certain factions may have more emphasis on the system than others.
