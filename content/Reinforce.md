# Reinforce
Restore a [[Member Count|member]] to this unit. Right click to automate.

## Details
Takes 10 seconds out of combat.

Cost is 75% of base units cost for Space Marines.
Cost is 50% of base units cost for Orks. (need to check after cost reduction from research)
Cost is 50% of *current* unit cost (taking the Noosphere Strength reduction into account) for Ad Mech.
Free for Necrons. (Is time different? faster on PM?)
