# [[Armour of Contempt]]
*Mechanic*
This unit absorbs extra damage when it loses a member, kills an enemy unit, or gains a Veterancy Level.

## Details
Every time a space marine infantry unit kills a model, loses a model, or levels up, it receives a shield that can absorb up to 150 points of damage per model.
A 3 model Eliminator squad get a 450pts shield, while 5 man squads like intercessors or terminator squads get 750pts shield.
Exceptions are: 
- Infiltrator: 100 per model
- Bladeguard: 200 per model. 
- Eradicator: 200 per model. 
- Astoran: 200?
- Ezrael: 170
- Jonah?

The research [[Burning Hatred]] from the [[Sanctum]] that increases that shield by 50% points was not active in the beta. Ezrael's aura was also not affecting the amount.
