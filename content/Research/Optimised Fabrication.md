##### Optimised Fabrication
*Research*
Reduces the production cost of the following structures:
- Cogitator Relay
- Cognis Weapon Platform
- Emanatus Field Generator
- Void Shield Generator
*Requires Network Strength II*
**120.0** sec.
=> Fortification Production Cost **-25%**
