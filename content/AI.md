# AI

> [!quote] Abilities
> Jan here, Game Director on DoW4. Since the question came up, here’s a bit of context on how our five difficulty settings work. We built the Al around Hard. That's the Al playing to its full ability, making the decisions it wants to make. On Normal, it makes some deliberately less optimal decisions and handles fewer things at once. Easy takes that a step further. Across those three settings, the difference is entirely in how well the Al plays. No hidden bonuses, no cheating.
> 
> Story and Insane are where we add some extra help: higher unit veterancy and extra resources for the player on Story, or for the Al on Insane.
