# Waaagh!

Increase to further unlock the Ork arsenal. Build Waaagh! [[Waaagh! Banner|Banners]] or deal damage to enemies to increase Waaagh!

## Details
=> +50% damage
=> +100% damage when near banner (50% extra)
=> 40 sec. duration

Stacks with Beastboss' Beastly Rage for an  extra 50% (10 sec. only).
