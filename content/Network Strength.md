# [[Network Strength]]

> [!question] How do I increase NS?
> Every #AdMech #Structure adds some points to the network if they are build within range of a networks connected to [[Dominus Forgeshrine]].
> 
> The first Strength level requires 6 such points, with starting HQ providing 3 already. Every Strength level after that requires 4 extra points.
>
>Example: HQ + 2x [[War Cohort Altar]] + link to natural [[Cognis Weapon Platform]] would give 3 + 2x3 + 1 point, reaching Network Strength II (10 points in total: 6 | 4).
> -- Artist

#### Network Strength IV
Reduces production costs for units from this structure. Increases damage dealt by units within the network.
  Damage Dealt: **+15%**

#### Network Strength VI
Provides greater benefits to structures within the Noosphere Network.

Unlocks structures:
- [[War Cohort Armoury]]

Unlocks research:
- Analytical Divination
- Battle Codifier Ill
- Greater Sicarian Cohort Expansion
- Greater Maniple Expansion
- Hardened Machine Spirit
- Maniple Expansion
- Repair Protocols

#### Network Strength VII
Provides greater benefits to structures within the Noosphere Network.

Unlocks units:
- Skorpius Disintegrator
