# Badges
## General
### War Is Eternal
Complete all main campaigns.
### Power Overwhelming
Acquire a total of 100000 Power.
### The Means Of War
Acquire a total of 100000 Requisition.
### Every Strength Leveraged
Spend a total of 200 Command Points.
### Foe Slayer
Kill a total of 1000 enemy units.
### Muster The Army
Produce a total of 500 units.
### The Inner Circle
Play in a closed playtest of Dawn of War IV.
### There Is Only War
Win a total of 20 Skirmish or Multiplayer matches.
### A Personal Touch
Create a custom scheme in the Painter.
### A New War Beckons
Complete the Prologue.
### A Decisive Victory
Win a Skirmish or Multiplayer match within 10 minutes a total of 10 times.
### We Shall Never Relent
Win a Skirmish match in which your team has fewer players than the enemy team.
### Know No Fear
Win a Skirmish or Multiplayer match while at least one ally is defeated.
### Praise The Motive Force
Spend at least 10,000 Power during a a single Skirmish or Multiplayer match.
### No Expense Spared
Spend at least 25,000 Requisition during a single Skirmish or Multiplayer match.
### Lightning Assault
Win a Skirmish or Multiplayer match within 5 minutes.
### Expanded Strikeforce
Spend at least 15 Deployment Points in The Last Stand.
### Hard-Won Vitality
Collect a total of 10 Medicae Supplies in The Last Stand.
### Heroic Resilience
Survive 1 wave without a Commander becoming incapacitated in The Last Stand.
### Survivalist
Prevail in The Last Stand.
### No Mercy, No Respite
Survive a total of 150 waves in The Last Stand.
### Foul Reflections
Kill the doppelganger of each Commander in The Last Stand.
### Armed And Armoured
Unlock all equipment or reach max level with a Commander in
The Last Stand.
## Space Marine
### Victory At All Cost
Complete the Space Marine campaign.
### No Threat Too Great
Defeat the C'tan Shard of the Void Dragon within 25 minutes of the start of **Grand**
**Engagement**.
### Deep Strike
Deploy a total of 5 Drop Pods.
### Deadly Precision
Kill a total of 25 enemies with a Captain's **Special Issue Ammunition** ability.
### Airborne Insertion
Deploy a total of 10 units with a Thunderhawk Relay.
### Death From Above
Kill an enemy unit with a deploying Drop Pod.
### Courage Of The Emperor
Destroy the solar harvester before reinforcements arrive in **Frozen Dominion**.
### Mechanised Assault
Complete **Tundra Raid** without producing any additional infantry units.
### No Ground Yielded
Win a Skirmish or Multiplayer match as Space Marines without using Retreat.
## Ork
### Da New Boss Is 'Ere!
Complete the Ork campaign.
### Speed Kultist
Complete **Da Race** within 25 minutes.
### Da 'Ead 'Unter
Deal a total of 500 damage with a Warboss' abilities.
### All Mobbed Up!
Upgrade a Slugga, Shoota, or Beast Snagga Boyz unit to maximum member size.
### Told Ya Dey Was Rubbish!
Defeat the enemy patrol before reaching the second rok in **Rok Hunt**.
### A Lesson In Humility
Incapacitate an enemy Commander with Gretchin.
## Necron
### A World In Darkness
Complete the Necron campaign.
### Terrifying Advance
Teleport a total of 20 Necron units.
### Ceaseless Destruction
Kill 5 enemy units during a single use of a Skorpekh Lord's **Eternal Madness** ability.
### A Great Dynasty
Expand the Power Matrix over a total of 100000m².
### Deathless Legions
Restore a total of 10 units using a Resurrection Gallery.
### Death Defeated
Restore a Necron unit with 1% HP remaining to full HP.
## Adeptus Mechanicus
### Unearthed And Unleashed
Complete the Adeptus Mechanicus campaign.
### Rad Saturated
Deal a total of 8000 damage with the **Rad-Bombardment** Stratagem.
### Beneficiary Of The Omnissiah
Achieve maximum Noosphere Network Strength in a Skirmish or Multiplayer match.
### Noospheric Augmentation
Achieve Noosphere Network Strength X a total of 10 times.
### Battlefield Reconnaissance
Use a Technoarcheologist's **Infoslave Skull** ability on a total of 100 enemies.
### Expert Explorator Cohort
Complete **The Signal** without losing any units.
### No Failure Accepted
Complete **Lock and Key** without losing a single captured strategic location.
### Disdain For The Flesh
Recover the unmanned Rogal Dorn Battle Tanks before aiding any Astra Militarum in **Hive City Counterattack**.
### All Is One Within The Machine
Connect all Resource Points on a map to the Noosphere Network in a Skirmish or Multiplayer match.
