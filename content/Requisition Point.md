# [[Requisition Point]]
*Resource Point*
- Provides Requisition
- Can be captured by infantry
- Upgrade to prevent enemy capture
---
Provides **+65** ![[req.png|20]]/min income when held.

---
> [!info] Necrons
> Necrons do not get any Requisition from Requisition Points. Instead, they get a reduction of production times for units.
