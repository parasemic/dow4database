# Retreat
Fall back to the nearest #production structure.
![[Retreat.png]]

## Details
After a delay of forced retreat, you can interrupt it (icon goes from red to yellow).
Each faction has a different delay:

| Faction | Delay |
| ------- | ----- |
| Ork     | 10    |
| Ad Mech | 10    |
| Necron  | 0     |
| SM      | 30    |
