# Unit Supply Cost
> [!tip] supply/upkeep
> Each unit (free to build or not) will cost you some resource over time. Think of it as having to feel your soldier, where they fight or not. Or paying to retain your mercenaries.
> SM/Ad Mech/Orkz pay some ![[req.png|20]] per minute, Necrons pay ![[dom.png|20]].
> 
> Only Ad Mech has a way to reduce that cost, through research. (-20%)
